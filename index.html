Research

I’m currently working on a project about gene isoforms (variations of mRNA) in cardiomyocytes and how they contribute to hypertrophic cardiomyopathy disease. The project utilizes a self-created long-read single-cell Iso-seq approach to analyze the data. I can’t wait to see the results, but in the meantime, I’ll keep coding… one bug at a time!
